# 05: Required slow tier

**What to build:** A change to anything that affects detector output can't reach a green `make check` without a green `make check-slow` on that same code. Today an agent can rewrite the detector and hand off after only the non-slow suite, never running the weak-stripe detection rate or the false-alarm rate (Codex adversarial review, finding 2). The slow tier also becomes reliable enough to require. Its wall-clock assertion is loosened so machine load can't fail it, and the real speed gates move to a separate benchmark target. Long runs are pushed into the background, because a foreground Bash call is killed at 10 minutes and the slow tier takes about 40 today. Because the slow tier becomes compulsory on every detector change, it is also shrunk to a regression check at fixed stored thresholds (about 6 minutes), and the full ticket-03-precision measurement moves to an on-demand target. See `../spec.md` for the owner's rulings of 2026-11-10.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A green `make check-slow` writes a stamp: a hash of the files that affect detector output (the detector, the generator, the calibration code; the list is defined once and documented). A red or interrupted run writes nothing.
- [ ] `make check` fails when those files differ from the stamped hash. The message names the changed files and says to run `make check-slow` in the background. Changes only to docs, the CLI, the report or tests outside the slow tier never require the slow tier.
- [ ] The stamp is local, git-ignored state. The file list and the stamp rule are protected surfaces in CLAUDE.md.
- [ ] Timing split (owner ruling): in `check-slow`, the per-frame timing test asserts only a loose limit (about 30 s for 1600×400) and records the measured time. Ticket 03's 10 s gate and ticket 13's 5 s target move to a new `make bench`, which is not part of `check` or `check-slow`. It takes the median of several runs and records commit, CPU, load average and versions, the way `feasibility-gate.json` records run info.
- [ ] Slow-tier shrink (owner ruling, 2026-11-10): the compulsory `check-slow` runs a regression check, not stripe-detector ticket 03 at full precision. It scores fresh, documented seeds (disjoint per `test_seed_sets_are_disjoint_and_unused`) at the **fixed thresholds stored by the last full measurement** (today 74.12 at 256×256, 105.80 at 400×1600), with no per-run calibration. Operating rule: at most 1% chance of a false red at the pessimistic end of the stored baseline's 95% CI, and at most 5% chance of passing (a) detection at m = 0.2 falling to 0.90, or (b) the 256×256 false-alarm rate rising to 4%. The plan derives the sizes and pass counts from exact binomial models. Starting point: 300 m = 0.2 frames needing ≥ 281 detected, and 800 stripe-free frames allowing ≤ 20 false alarms, at 256×256. Add a small 400×1600 m = 0.2 detection set across all ten cases, sized by the same rule; full-frame false alarms stay with the full run. The 400×1600 timing frames assert their verdicts, not just their time. Target wall clock about 6 minutes, measured and recorded in Comments. A detector change that moves the score scale is expected to go red here; the fix is to run the full measurement, which re-stores the thresholds and baseline rates, never to edit the pass counts. The full ticket-03 measurement (`tests/feasibility.py`, about 39 min) stays an on-demand target outside `check` and `check-slow`, required at milestones where detector output is meant to move (stripe-detector 05, 07, 13). The seeds, sample sizes, pass counts and stored thresholds are protected surfaces.
- [ ] A PreToolUse hook, adapted from `~/dev/polsigns/.claude/hooks/require_background_gate.py`, refuses `make check-slow` and `make bench` in the foreground. It allows anything it cannot parse, sees through `bash -lc` wrappers, and has an explicit trailing-comment override.
- [ ] Proof: after a planted no-op edit to a detector file, `make check` is red with the stale-stamp message. After a green `make check-slow`, `make check` is green again. A foreground `make check-slow` is refused by the hook. The plants are not committed.
