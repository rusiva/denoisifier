That count is not determined by the direction. Along a finite row the fork term sweeps `atan2` over less than π: at the default 256 px width, P 10 and 50 px offset, from -158.7° to -21.3° above (0.76 π) and the mirror below. The top-minus-bottom phase change is therefore about 1.53 π·d, not 2 π·d. Each row's maxima count is a difference of floors that depends on `phase0` (a random draw, `synthetic.py:345`) and on where the row's ends fall. Worked through: count(top) - count(bottom) = [floor(a - 0.118) - floor(a)] - [floor(b - 0.882) - floor(b)], which is -1, 0 or +1 for d = +1 depending on the seed. So correct code fails on some seeds, and the plan's seed policy forbids re-picking them. A wider row only brings the sweep close to π. It never removes the floor ambiguity. → Plan change: assert something continuous. Either (a) use the winding of the noise-free analytic phase around a rectangle centred on the fork, or (b) take the unwrapped phase change along the top row minus the bottom row, divided by 2π and rounded (with a wide row, e.g. 400x1600, the value is about 0.98·d).
