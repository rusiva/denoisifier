Findings:
- [high] The adaptive union rule does not preserve the requested alarm probability (.scratch/stripe-detector/handoffs/11-plan.md:90-100)
  Missed by both documents: selecting the fork threshold and then ranking conditional break scores on the same frames invalidates the ordinary order-statistic argument. Consider independent continuous break/fork evidence above the floors. With n=300 and r=0.05, k_f=7 excludes six calibration frames; the break threshold becomes the ninth-largest of the remaining 294. The new-frame union probability is 7/301 + (294/301)(9/295) = 5.305%, exceeding 5%. At the minimum supported request, 2/301, it becomes 0.994% instead of 0.664%. These are analytic counterexamples, not measured detector rates. Ties or binding floors might mask this on the initial dataset but cannot justify the public rule.
  Recommendation: Choose OQ2(a): preallocate r/2 to each scalar using ordinary marginal order statistics, retaining the floors. Alternatively, establish a valid joint calibration method before adopting adaptive allocation. Document marginal-over-calibration coverage separately from uncertainty in the shipped thresholds.
- [high] Frame maxima cannot calibrate the promised number of fake defects (.scratch/stripe-detector/handoffs/11-plan.md:160)
  The calibration targets whether a frame has any fake, while AC2 counts every reported break and fork. Identical per-frame maxima can accompany very different multiplicities: 5% of frames with four fakes means 0.20 fakes per frame. The stored scalars discard that distinction. Moreover, false_alarm_tolerance models a beta-binomial count of alarmed frames under one order-statistic threshold, not clustered defect counts under two thresholds. Its actual central 99.5% interval here is [3,33], so the proposed test accepts 33 defects in 300 frames. Review N2's suggestion to label this an approximation does not establish either its coverage or false-red budget.
  Recommendation: Resolve the estimand before freezing the API. To retain the ticket's count requirement, calibrate and validate per-frame defect counts with an explicitly justified count-risk bound, accounting for threshold selection. If a frame-event probability is chosen instead, explicitly amend the requirement and use a valid event-count test; do not reuse its bound for total defects.
- [high] Full-frame calibration does not establish conservative coverage for crops or other settings (.scratch/stripe-detector/handoffs/11-plan.md:307-310)
  Both calibration and held-out negatives use full-frame stripes at depth 0.5 and the same ten Period/Angle pairs. OQ4 nevertheless calls this conservative for crops. That does not follow from area: the finders recompute domain-relative magnitude floors and median carriers, and analysis adds searches between compatible regions. Partial regions, weaker stripes and crops change those operations rather than merely removing search locations. This is an unsupported extrapolation, not a measured failure. Review N3 identifies custom stripe thresholds and binning as outside coverage, but prose alone still leaves those supported runtime options silently using the default defect calibration.
  Recommendation: Restrict the initial claim to the declared synthetic recipe and mixture. Before broadening it, independently test partial/multiple regions, weaker stripes and actual crops. Add Report warnings for detectable calibration mismatches, including resolved stripe threshold/calibration, binning, shape and clipping; checking the stored stripe digest alone is insufficient.
