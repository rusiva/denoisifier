# 17: Region growth and phase steps under phase breaks

**What to build:** Striped Regions that hold their extent, Period and Angle when a Phase Break crosses them, by fixing the score path rather than repairing the report after it. Ticket 09 (scope C, `.scratch/stripe-detector/handoffs/09-critic-2.md`) left the detector's score path untouched and repairs only what it can after the threshold. Measured at BASE `b7c6731` on 600 break frames (`.scratch/stripe-detector/handoffs/09-prototyper-1.md`, Q2):

- **Overgrowth.** A break dims the depth along its line, so one stripe set seeds as two candidates whose seed areas straddle the dimmed band. Their median depth drops (0.46 to 0.30 and 0.21 at seed 109404), the grow level `_GROW_FRACTION * median` drops with it, and the merged bbox ends 1.1-1.4 Periods too tall on 21 of 600 frames (P >= 5, jump >= 3π/4, near-horizontal lines).
- **Angle bias from `_phase_steps`.** It is a weighted mean of neighbour products, so the blurred jump folds into the frequency at the candidate and merge steps (candidate Angle -7.68° against -10°). Ticket 09 re-measures Angle away from a *found* break; a break below the finder's limits (`|jump| < π/4` or low evidence) keeps the bias.
- **A half-region below the threshold.** When only one half of a broken region scores over the threshold, the report has one region covering one side; ticket 09 bridges only reported regions.
- Whichever of ticket 09's slit fill and Period/Angle re-measure its prototype dropped. (Prototype P1 v2 kept both; see ticket 09's Comments for the measured numbers.)

`_phase_steps` feeds the score through its candidate (`analysis.py:537`) and merge (`:641`) callers, and `_GROW_FRACTION` / `_MERGE_GAP_PERIODS` were tuned on `REGION_CALIBRATION_SEEDS` (ticket 05). Any fix is a detector change that moves scores: re-prove ticket 05's criteria, then `make measure`, a calibration re-store (CLAUDE.md, the re-store procedure) and the held-out tests. Suggested to ride with ticket 16.
