# The break and Fork thresholds are order statistics of stored per-frame evidence on break- and Fork-free striped frames

The package ships, next to the stripe Calibration Set (ADR 0002), a second stored set: 300 generated break- and Fork-free striped 400x1600 frames (full-frame stripes at Modulation Depth 0.5, Period and Angle from the ten cases, scorched Mottle, about 100 photons at the peak, EMCCD noise, 30 spike events, unclipped), each with two scalars read on the regions reported at the stored 1% stripe Threshold, unbinned: S_b, the smallest break threshold at which the break pass finds no Phase Break (per reported region, the E of its first best line when that line passes the jump and spread rules, else 0; per bridge domain between two compatible regions, the largest min(E_1..E_j) over the lines j of the greedy sequence that split the two regions (meet the side shares); the largest of these), and S_f, the largest Fork candidate's E (0 when there is none). At a requested rate r the break threshold is the k-th largest S_b and the Fork threshold the k-th largest S_f, k = floor((r/2)(n + 1)) each, never below the floors `_BREAK_MIN_EVIDENCE` = 0.1 and `_FORK_MIN_EVIDENCE` = 0.3 (the conservative thresholds of tickets 09 and 10); a break or Fork is reported only when its E is strictly above its threshold. The default rate is 0.05 (about one frame in 20 with a fake). Splitting r into r/2 per kind bounds the share of frames with either fake by r (union bound), without modelling how the two kinds correlate. We chose stored per-frame evidence over one stored number per kind for the reasons of ADR 0002 (any rate without a new run; a changed scalar traceable to a frame), and the floors over thresholds that fall to 0 on frames with no candidate, so that a calibration with no evidence never makes the finders looser than the tested 09/10 behaviour. The file is separate from the stripe file (a different Calibration Set; the stripe digest and reproduction stay untouched) and records the stripe calibration digest it was read at, so a stripe re-store without a break/Fork re-run fails `make check`.

## Consequences

- **The floors bind.** On the 300 Calibration Set frames S_b = S_f = 0 everywhere (P1: 0 of 200 prototype frames above 0), so the thresholds are the floors 0.1 and 0.3 at every valid rate (r >= 2/(n + 1) = 0.66%), and the rate option changes nothing on the shipped calibration. The claim is the measured rate below, not a tuned threshold. Ties at exactly the floor cannot report (strict `>`).
- **Found bounds reported.** S_b is the largest of each region's first accepted line's E and, in each bridge domain, the largest min(E_1..E_j) over the splitting lines, so a frame with S_b <= T_b finds, and so reports, no break; S_f bounds the reported Forks when no break is found. A reported Fork on a frame with S_f <= T_f needs a found break (the post-break search reads the response with breaks explained away). The held-out test checks these implications on every frame.
- **AC 2 count model.** The held-out bound on the total count of fake breaks and Forks in 300 frames is the one-sided point at `CALIBRATION_MAX_FALSE_RED` of the single-order-statistic model at r (betabinom(300, 15, 286): 29). It is conservative for frames with a fake (their rate is at most r) and an approximation for the count, which can exceed frames with a fake; that excess is covered by the prototype's multiplicity rule (total fakes at most 1.5x frames with fakes, 0 and 0 on the calibration recipe). The model and the bound were fixed before the held-out run.
- **Measured (held-out, ticket 11 close).** `make check-held-out` at `98db7ff`, `.gate/held-out-break-fork.json`, on the 300 frames `BREAK_FORK_FREE_HELD_OUT_SEEDS` (114_000-114_299) of the calibration recipe at the stored thresholds (0.1, 0.3): every frame Striped, 0 fake Phase Breaks and 0 fake Forks (bound 29), 0 frames with a fake (exact 95% upper limit 1.22%), no per-case alarm, no implication violated, S_b > T_b and S_f > T_f on 0 frames. Detection on 100 break frames (09's AC 2 geometry, jumps of pi/2 or more) and 100 Fork frames (10's AC 2): 100/100 and 100/100 found (bar 95), 100/100 each at the floors, 0 lost by calibration. The full 300-frame reproduction of the stored calibration matched every scalar.
- **Scope of the claim.** Calibrated on the regions reported at the stored 1% stripe Threshold, unbinned, 400x1600, full-frame stripes at depth 0.5. `threshold=`, `false_alarm_rate=`, `calibration=`, `bin_factors`, crops, partial regions and weaker stripes change the regions and domains the finders see; the mechanics are the same, the claim does not cover them. Measured on 200 prototype frames each at the floors: full-frame stripes at depth 0.2, 1 frame with a fake (0.5%, one break, E 0.132); a partial region over half the frame at depth 0.5, 1 frame (0.5%) with 3 fake breaks, all in a false second region at the frame edge (the fake-region kind; ticket 17); the calibration recipe at a 5% stripe rate, 0 frames.
- **Fork frames are not in the set.** The held-out frames have no Forks, so fake breaks next to close Fork pairs or Forks near an edge (ticket 10, ticket 18) are not measured.
- **Recalibration.** Real-frame recalibration of these thresholds is deferred; `_BreakForkCalibration.from_json` is the seam. The evidence definition is versioned (`"evidence": "break-fork-v1"`); a change to it, to `_local_phase.py`, the break pass, the Fork searches, the shared front half, or a stripe re-store re-runs the break/Fork driver through the re-store procedure (CLAUDE.md).
- **Gates.** `make check-slow` re-runs the calibration routine on the first 60 frames (6 per case) and requires every scalar to reproduce exactly; `make check-held-out` reproduces all 300 and runs the held-out fake count and detection checks, at ticket 11's close and at `make measure` milestones.
